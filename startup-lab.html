FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this a paid job?
No. This is a project-based career development program. Future paid internship or employment opportunities may be considered for outstanding participants, but they are not guaranteed.
Will I receive a certificate?
Qualified participants who complete required sessions and deliverables may receive a Completion Letter based on actual participation and performance.
Is this related to OPT or H1B?
This program does not guarantee OPT, CPT, STEM OPT, H1B, or any immigration benefit. Students should consult their school DSO or immigration attorney for visa-related questions.
What kind of work will I do?
Participants may work on research, SEO, content strategy, data analysis, growth marketing, product testing, and business development tasks related to MovingCOST.ai.
Who is the program best for?
It is best for students who want real startup exposure, practical project experience, and a stronger portfolio.
