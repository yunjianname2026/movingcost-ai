Founder Letter

A Letter From the Founder

Welcome to the AI Startup Career Lab.

This program was created for students who want more than another online course. In the AI era, knowledge alone is not enough. What matters is whether you can understand real problems, work with a team, use modern tools, communicate clearly, and build something that creates value.

MovingCOST.ai is a real AI mobility platform. Through this lab, selected students will work on practical assignments across research, data, content, growth, product, and business development. You will not only learn how a startup is built — you will participate in the building process.

Our goal is simple: help motivated students gain real project exposure, build portfolio-ready deliverables, and develop founder-level thinking.

This is the beginning of a long-term talent and startup ecosystem.

Welcome to the founding cohort.

Founder, MovingCOST.ai

Project Tracks

Project Tracks

Six tracks. Pick one primary focus — or explore adjacent areas across the cohort.

AI Content & SEO
What you will learn
How to research mobility topics, map search intent, and structure content for an AI platform.
Example tasks
City guide research, keyword mapping, content briefs, on-page SEO recommendations.
Possible deliverables
Content brief pack, SEO audit memo, editorial calendar proposal.
Data & Market Research
What you will learn
How to collect, organize, and interpret relocation and cost-of-living data.
Example tasks
City cost comparisons, competitor scans, data validation, market sizing notes.
Possible deliverables
Research dataset summary, market analysis brief, competitor landscape report.
Growth Marketing
What you will learn
How early-stage products test channels, messaging, and community outreach.
Example tasks
Campaign ideas, social content plans, referral concepts, landing page feedback.
Possible deliverables
Growth experiment proposal, channel strategy memo, campaign performance review.
Business Development
What you will learn
How startups identify partners, sponsors, and service providers in a mobility ecosystem.
Example tasks
Partner research, outreach lists, sponsor mapping, partnership pitch drafts.
Possible deliverables
Partner prospect list, sponsor research deck, BD outreach plan.
Product & User Experience
What you will learn
How to evaluate product flows, test features, and translate user feedback into improvements.
Example tasks
Usability reviews, feature testing, user interview notes, UX improvement proposals.
Possible deliverables
Product feedback memo, UX audit, feature recommendation brief.
AI Workflow & Automation
What you will learn
How AI tools can responsibly support research, content, marketing, and operations.
Example tasks
Workflow mapping, prompt design, automation experiments, tool evaluation.
Possible deliverables
AI workflow playbook, automation proposal, tool comparison report.

Recognition Policy

Completion Letter & Recommendation Policy

Qualified participants who complete the required sessions and deliverables may receive a Completion Letter.

Outstanding participants may be considered for a Recommendation Letter based on actual performance, quality of work, reliability, communication, and contribution to the project.

Recommendation letters are issued only after successful completion of program requirements and are based on demonstrated performance throughout the cohort.

No document is guaranteed. All letters must reflect actual participation and performance.

AI Use Policy

AI Use Policy

Participants are encouraged to use AI tools responsibly for research, drafting, analysis, ideation, and workflow improvement. However, all submitted work must be reviewed, edited, verified, and owned by the participant.

  • Do not submit unverified AI output as final work.
  • Cite sources when using external data.
  • Do not fabricate research, metrics, companies, or contacts.
  • Protect confidential project information.
  • Use AI as a tool, not a replacement for judgment.
Professional Conduct

Professional Conduct

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a job?
No. This is a project-based career development program.
Is this an internship?
It is a project-based startup lab. Any future internship or employment opportunity would be separate and based on business needs, eligibility, and performance.
Will I get a Completion Letter?
Qualified participants may receive one after completing required sessions and deliverables.
Can I use this on my resume?
Participants may describe completed project work accurately and truthfully.
Does this support OPT/CPT/H1B?
This program does not guarantee or provide immigration benefits. Students should consult their school DSO or immigration attorney.
What language is used?
English is the primary working language. Chinese support may be available for selected sessions.
